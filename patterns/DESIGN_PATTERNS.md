# Design patterns continuity policy

Use the smallest pattern that preserves the authorized requirement, units, guards, and trajectory under declared faults. Before sharing work, record the pattern ID, baseline, target runtime, requirement → model → function → test, and the owner of each effect. Declare ordering, deadlines, resource limits, loss, and retry at each concurrent boundary.

**Admit effects at commit.** Bind each proposal to an immutable snapshot, write scope, and governing epoch. Recheck authority and guards atomically with the write, advance the epoch on change, and reject stale proposals without effect. Declare Primary, Alternate, Contingency, Emergency, and Terminal capabilities. Bound retries, reconcile uncertain acknowledgments before replay, and require authorized restart after Terminal.

**Qualify the use.** Match evidence to the exact runtime and scope. Exercise nominal, denied, stale, and relevant fault and regression paths. HOLD the affected obligation when a mandatory witness is missing or a contract fails. Record the reason and release condition. The designated authority approves deployment.

**Evidence scope.** B/C/S IDs expand to `RDP-*`. Their leaf manifests bind local software witnesses. Reproduction needs the upstream `hmt_rdp` package and campaign tooling. PostgreSQL IDs expand to `PG-PAT-0xx`. The PostgreSQL release manifest expects `src/hmt_postgresql_design_patterns.egg-info/PKG-INFO`. This bundle ships matching bytes under `src/hmt_postgresql_design_patterns.egg-info-PKG-INFO`, making exact-path verification incomplete. Its [verification report](database/postgresql/VERIFICATION_REPORT.md) leaves native authentication, wire transport, independent-backend contention, and power-loss recovery unqualified. The [pinned retry source](resilience_ooda/sources/resilience_ooda.zip) retains the simulator coverage and original live Tenacity evidence. The [OODA rollout](resilience_ooda/README.md) qualifies its selected profiles with local callbacks. Target handoff and cancellation require measured bounds or independent enforcement.
